# testbed: product memos on the 5 transfers

## Description

The accounting demo narrates wallet B's incoming transfers as merchant sales.
The transfer memos must look like customer purchases (products bought) instead
of `transfer #N`. The memo is encrypted in the transfer note and decrypted by
the scanner, so it becomes the Beancount narration.

## Design

`scripts/scenario.sh` — replace the transfer loop's memo with a coffee-shop
catalog, keeping the amounts (10/25/40/60/100) and the `--show-sender` toggle:

```bash
memos=(
  "2x Espresso"
  "1x Latte, 1x Croissant, 1x Orange Juice"
  "2x Sandwich, 2x Coffee"
  "1x Cake, 3x Latte"
  "Catering: 10x Coffee, 10x Pastry"
)
i=0
for amt in 10 25 40 60 100; do
  flag=""; (( i % 2 == 0 )) && flag="--show-sender"
  ./op.sh transfer --from A --to B --amount "$amt" --memo "${memos[$i]}" $flag
  i=$((i+1))
done
```

Notes:
- Shield/unshield have no on-chain memo (the SDK's shield/unshield calls do
  not accept one); the ledger writer narrates them by `kind` instead.
- The transfer op already supports `--memo` (`demo/src/ops/transfer.ts`).
- Update the scenario snippet in `docs/testbed/spec.md`.

## Acceptance Criteria

- `scripts/scenario.sh` runs end to end with the 5 product memos.
- `out/steps.jsonl` records the product memos on the transfer steps.
- The scanner's B events carry the product memos (verified by the scanner
  tests / Fava narration).
